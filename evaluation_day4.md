## AI Evaluation Tasks — Day 4

---

### Task 1: Ranking Responses

Question: Why do objects fall?

Response A: Objects fall because of gravity.
Response B: Objects fall due to gravity, which pulls them toward Earth.
Response C: Objects fall due to gravitational force that attracts masses toward each other, especially toward Earth's center.

Ranking:

1. Response C
2. Response B
3. Response A

Explanation:

* Response C provides the most complete reasoning
* Response B is correct but less detailed
* Response A is too shallow

### Task 2: Hallucination Detection

Question: What is the capital of Germany?

Response: The capital of Germany is Munich.

Evaluation:

* Correctness: Incorrect
* Issue: Factual error (hallucination)

Correct Answer: Berlin

Explanation:
The response provides false information, which is considered hallucination in AI outputs.
